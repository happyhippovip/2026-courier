## 1. Executive Summary & The Economic Invariant

In late 2026, as autonomous agent runtimes matured from experimental prototypes into production-grade multi-agent engines, the decisive challenge transitioned from technical viability to **economic durability**: establishing lawful, repeatable, high-margin revenue streams that fund continuous computational capacity while preserving fail-closed safety and strict zero-spend boundaries.

Courier enforces a foundational **Self-Funding Loop**:
$$\text{User Value} \longrightarrow \text{Sellable Product} \longrightarrow \text{Verified Revenue} \longrightarrow \text{Human-Approved Reinvestment} \longrightarrow \text{Expanded Capacity \& Speed}$$

### Core Economic Directives:
1. **The Self-Funding Milestone Ladder:**
   $$\text{€1 Verified} \longrightarrow \text{€239/mo Baseline Surplus} \longrightarrow \text{€439/mo AI Supportable} \longrightarrow \text{€1,000/mo Sustainable Surplus} \longrightarrow \text{Enterprise Scale}$$
2. **First Euro Principle:** Secure the earliest legitimate, independently verified transaction to validate willingness to pay (WTP) before committing heavy capital or compute to expansive roadmaps.
3. **Strict Verification Standard (No Fantasy Economics):** Revenue is defined exclusively as cash settled in an authorized account. Unsigned offers, pipeline estimates, web traffic, social metrics, or agent self-certifications represent zero economic progress.
4. **Fail-Closed Spend Boundary (€0.00 EUR Autonomous Limit):** Operating agents possess zero authority to incur billing, upgrade tiers, sign contracts, or perform outbound solicitation without explicit, branch-local human gate authorization.

## 4. The Stage-Budget Ladder & Go-To-Market Protocol

To eliminate speculative spending, every commercial initiative must traverse Courier's disciplined stage gate:

```
[Stage 0: Research] 
   └── Objective: Pain point identification, competitor pricing audit, and offer framing. (Zero cost)
[Stage 1: Near-Zero-Cost Validation] 
   └── Objective: Pre-orders, waitlists, inbound landing conversion, or technical demo verification. (Zero spend)
[Stage 2: Bounded Test Execution] 
   └── Objective: Deliver first transaction to an early adopter; verify unit economics and delivery time. (Budget: < €50)
[Stage 3: Validated Pilot / Cohort] 
   └── Objective: Execute 3-5 repeatable sales; achieve cumulative €239 surplus milestone.
[Stage 4: Automated Scale & Reinvestment] 
   └── Objective: Expand commercial licensing; reinvest net cash into dedicated compute and infrastructure.
```

### Stop-Loss and Kill Triggers:
- If a candidate yields zero WTP evidence within its allocated time window, it is parked immediately.
- If delivery cost or human intervention exceeds €0.60 per €1.00 gross revenue, the pricing structure must be refactored or killed.
- Sunk costs must never dictate prioritization: superior opportunities immediately preempt inferior workloads.
