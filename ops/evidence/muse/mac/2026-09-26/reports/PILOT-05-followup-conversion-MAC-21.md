# PILOT-05 FOLLOWUP + DEMO-TO-PILOT CONVERSION (MUSE-MAC-21, 2026-09-26)

## FOLLOWUP_MESSAGE (day 14, sent with the evaluation)
Subject: Your 2-week Courier pilot — results
Body:
"These 2 weeks: [N] tasks ran in [scope]. [K] mornings you resumed without
reconstructing (saved ~[M] min vs your baseline of [B1] min). Humans stepped
in [H] times, each time at a gate you approved: [list]. Nothing ran twice,
nothing was lost — proof cards attached.
Do you want to continue with [next scope]? Reply YES for 2 more weeks at
[price], or NO and I delete everything on [date] and send confirmation.
— [operator]"
Rules: numbers from cards only; no adjectives about the numbers; NO always
offered first-class; deletion promise kept within 48h of NO.

## DEMO_TO_PILOT_CONVERSION_FLOW
1. DEMO (60s, RV05 peers own variants): problem -> task -> work -> verify ->
   continue -> restart note -> result -> proof card. Ends with: "That was a
   recording of real behavior [or live run]. Want to see YOUR task do this?"
2. WITNESSED TEST (RV18, in front of them, ~2 min): the conversion event.
   PASS -> "That is what a pilot week looks like every day. Shall we scope
   yours?" FAIL -> "That saved us both a bad pilot. I will tell you when
   this check passes." No pitch after a FAIL, ever.
3. SCOPING (same session if PASS): B1-B5 baseline questions (PILOT-02),
   handshake fields, date for day 0.
4. DAY 0: onboarding script (PILOT-02), first proof card delivered.
5. DAY 14: evaluation + FOLLOWUP_MESSAGE above.
Conversion metric that matters: witnessed-PASS -> scoped-pilot rate, and
FAIL -> later-return rate. Track both, publish neither as marketing.

## Key-promise handling (honesty rule)
"Back to your work the next day without reconstructing" is the promise the
pilot TESTS (S1), never a claim made before day 14. Until S1 holds for a
real customer, all outward copy says: "we are testing whether Courier gives
you your mornings back" — present tense of experiment, not of achievement.
