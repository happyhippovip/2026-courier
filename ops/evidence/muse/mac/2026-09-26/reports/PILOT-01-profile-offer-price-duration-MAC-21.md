# PILOT-01 PROFILE, OFFER, PRICE, DURATION (MUSE-MAC-21, 2026-09-26)

MODE=NON_CODE_PREP. No fake customers, no fake revenue, no Product Shell.

## IDEAL_FIRST_PILOT_PROFILE (first 5)
- Solo knowledge worker or 2-person team, Mac-based, with recurring
  multi-step computer work (inbox triage, weekly reporting, research
  collation — cf. RV06 workflow types).
- Pain: loses 30+ min/day reconstructing "where was I yesterday".
- Technical enough to watch a 2-minute terminal demo, non-technical enough
  not to ask for architecture. Has authority to approve a small pilot spend.
- Excluded: anyone needing Windows, SLA guarantees, 24/7 support, or
  compliance certification (all unproven — Z10 class 4).

## PILOT_OFFER
"Two weeks, one scoped project. We run a witnessed acceptance test in front
of you first — if it prints FAIL, there is no invoice and the failure report
is yours. If it prints PASS, we run your scoped work for two weeks and
deliver a proof card for every task: what ran, what was verified, how many
times a human had to step in."

## PILOT_PRICE_EXPERIMENT
- No tiers, no seats, no platform fee (nothing billable exists to meter).
- Experiment: fixed pilot fee for the 2 weeks, set per pilot (scope-sized),
  invoiced manually AFTER the witnessed PASS. Price is a learning instrument:
  record willingness-to-pay + which proof (card? zero-intervention count?
  next-day continuity?) justified it.
- Rule: money is a REAL_GATE — explicit approval per pilot, revocable,
  audit record. No auto-renewal talk until GAP-3 closes.

## PILOT_DURATION
- 2 weeks wall-clock: day 0 witnessed test + scope handshake; days 1-10
  scoped runs with proof cards; day 11-14 evaluation against SUCCESS_CRITERIA
  (PILOT-03). Short enough to bound risk, long enough to hit at least one
  overnight/next-day continuity event — the promise being tested.
