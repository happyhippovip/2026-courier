## NO_PERMISSION_SPAM_EXPLANATION

(Note: no permission-rules source doc found — grounded in plan §§1, 5 stop rule + Goal Contract.)
"You confirm your goal once — what should happen, what shouldn't, and where a human must decide. After that Courier only comes back to you at real boundaries: a decision only you can make, money, a permission, a safety or legal line, or a conflict it can't resolve. Everything else it handles or marks honestly as unknown. Fewer popups by design — and the one confirmation at the start is never skipped." Last sentence is load-bearing: the Goal Contract confirmation is mandatory, not skippable (plan §5).
