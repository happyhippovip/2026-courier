## Experiment framing (publishable now)
"Paid pilot, fixed scope, fixed duration. You pay for setup + a
two-week pilot window, not per promise. If the proof card doesn't show
unattended chained execution, you don't continue."

## What to measure (not what to charge)
1. Do visitors request the pilot? (CTA clicks on /pilot)
2. Which scope do they bring? (free-text task description + workspace size)
3. What proof do they ask for first? (duplicates? crashes? cost?)
4. What stops them? (exit survey, one question)

## Forbidden until proven
- Per-task prices, per-seat prices, SLA percentages, uptime figures,
  "enterprise ready", customer logos, ROI multiples.

## Kill criteria for the experiment
If <5 qualified pilot inquiries in 30 days after launch, pricing is not
the problem — revisit demo proof (RV05) and workflow candidates (RV06)
before touching copy.
