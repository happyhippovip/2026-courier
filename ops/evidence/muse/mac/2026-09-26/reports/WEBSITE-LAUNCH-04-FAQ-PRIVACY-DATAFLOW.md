# FAQ + Privacy Summary + Data Flow Explanation

## FAQ (launch set — each answer claim-checked)

1. **What is Courier?** A system we're building so work you start with AI continues where you stopped — with a verifiable record of what happened. Currently in proof stage; first access via accompanied pilots.
2. **Is it available now?** No. The core is being proven step by step (public roadmap on `/company`). You can join the pilot waitlist.
3. **Does it replace my AI tools?** No. It sits across tools, workers and sessions and keeps continuity + evidence. It doesn't compete on model count (plan §2, internal — phrased externally as "works with your tools").
4. **What does it cost?** Nothing yet — pilot pricing is a labeled experiment (49–99 € orientation, agreed in writing). No subscriptions exist.
5. **What data do you touch?** Only what's written in your pilot agreement: goal data, execution records, evidence. Sensitive/private data stays private by default; nothing is published or shared without written consent. Details: privacy summary below.
6. **Do I need to install something?** Not yet — pilots are accompanied and manual; there is no installer and none is promised (plan §15, Gate 7 LOCKED).
7. **What happens when Courier doesn't know something?** It marks it UNKNOWN and stops at the real boundary (your decision, money, permission, safety) instead of guessing or quietly passing. (Design principle — SAFE.)
8. **How do I know it worked?** You get a proof card per finished piece: goal, result, checks performed, evidence references, open unknowns, human interventions. Sample card on `/approach` is labeled SAMPLE until real gate evidence exists.
9. **Who is behind Courier?** Mission + philosophy on `/company`. No invented team, customers, or partners (register C13).
10. **Can I invest / partner / grant-fund?** Contact email; one-pager with architecture, roadmap, evidence status and measured numbers (real ones only) on request. No valuation or partnership claims on the public page.

## PRIVACY_SUMMARY (public, plain words; full policy needs legal review before pilot 1 — plan §19)

- We process only data named in your written pilot agreement (goal content, execution records, measurement notes).
- Storage location, retention, deletion, involved providers and permissions are agreed per pilot, in writing, before start.
- No passwords, keys, payment data, or ID documents via website forms — ever.
- No sale of data, no marketing reuse of pilot content, no public case study without separate written consent.
- Deletion: on request at pilot end; execution evidence needed for our own proof claims is anonymized or deleted per agreement.
- This summary is not legal advice and doesn't replace the per-pilot agreement (plan §19: product plan ≠ legal/tax advice). BLOCKER before first paid pilot: payment path, privacy notices, trade/tax registration questions — tracked as launch blockers, not website copy.

## DATA_FLOW_EXPLANATION (diagram + words for `/approach`)

```
You → Goal confirmation → Courier control → Workers / Models / Tools
→ Results → Checks → Proof card → You (Arbeitet / Braucht dich / Fertig)
```

Words: "Your goal goes in once. Courier coordinates the steps across tools and sessions. Every result is checked against your goal before anything continues. You see three states: it's working, it needs you (only at real boundaries), it's done — each 'done' comes with a proof card. Data stays within the scope you agreed; providers involved are named in your pilot agreement." Nothing here claims a shipped implementation — verbs describe the designed flow, page-labeled accordingly until Gates 2–4 PASS.
