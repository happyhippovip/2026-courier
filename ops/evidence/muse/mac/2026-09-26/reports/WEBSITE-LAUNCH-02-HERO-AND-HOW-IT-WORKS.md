## HOW_IT_WORKS (4 steps, future tense, `/approach`)

Framing line: "How Courier will work — currently under proof, step by step."

1. **You confirm the goal — once.** You describe the outcome, the boundaries, and where a human must decide (money, permissions, safety). After that, no re-explaining. (Grounded: plan §5 Goal Contract. Design principle — SAFE.)
2. **Courier does the work in traceable steps.** Each task leaves a record: what was asked, what ran, what came back, what was checked. (Grounded: plan §4 ledger truth. Approach, not guarantee — SAFE with "leaves a record" tied to proof-card preview.)
3. **It asks only at real boundaries.** A decision only you can make, a payment, a permission, a safety or legal line — otherwise it continues on its own. (Grounded: plan §1 stop rule. C4 as design principle — SAFE.)
4. **It shows its work.** Every finished piece gets a compact proof card: goal, result, checks, evidence, open unknowns, human interventions. Unknowns stay marked unknown — never quietly passed. (Grounded: plan §10–11. SAFE as "what you'll receive"; the example card must be labeled SAMPLE until Gate 1 PASS.)
