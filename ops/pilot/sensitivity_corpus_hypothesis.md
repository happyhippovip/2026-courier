# Courier Sensitivity Corpus Hypothesis

**Private Hypothesis:**
Will the historical tuple of `(Model, Processor, Prompt, Environment)` -> `Outcome / Incident Data` become a valuable proprietary asset?

**Rationale:**
As AI models evolve, they exhibit distinct failure modes, regressions, and "sensitivities" to specific phrasing, timing, or context limits. A corpus mapping exact inputs to documented failures/successes across time could be used to:
1. Pre-flight check prompts for likely failure.
2. Automatically select the most reliable provider for a specific task cluster.
3. Sell vulnerability databases to enterprises.

**Falsification Test:**
1. Collect 50 tuples of Courier failures.
2. Attempt to predict the failure of the 51st run based purely on the corpus.
3. If prediction accuracy is < 80%, the corpus is not dense enough or the models are too non-deterministic to form a moat.

**Verdict:**
Do not actively sell this. Quietly accumulate the Ledger data during the EUR390 pilot. Re-evaluate at 1,000 tasks.
