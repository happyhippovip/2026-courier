# Courier Commercialization Strategy

**1. Buyer:** 
Founders, Technical Leads, or Ops Managers overwhelmed by manual, repetitive cognitive tasks.

**2. Pain:** 
High cognitive load, context switching, brittle manual processes.

**3. Trigger:** 
A critical team member goes on leave, or a project scales beyond manual capacity.

**4. Offer:** 
EUR149 Continuity Audit (Entry) -> EUR390 7-Day Pilot (Core).

**5. Deliverable:** 
Concrete Customer Result Objects (e.g., Code repair, Data extraction, Decision briefs).

**6. Price Test:** 
Can we consistently sell EUR149 without a sales call?

**7. Kill Criteria:** 
If 0 sales out of 10 targeted pitches, abandon current pricing/offer structure. No outreach permitted yet; must rely on inbound or immediate network.
