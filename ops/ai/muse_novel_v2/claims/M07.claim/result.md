QUESTION=How can a beginner understand WHY a result should be trusted in ten seconds?

WHAT_WE_ASSUMED=We assumed that providing a perfectly accurate final answer is enough to earn trust, and that if a user wants proof, they can just read the agent's execution logs.

WHAT_EVIDENCE_SAYS=Beginners judge reliability based on "observable effort"—if they see a human work on a problem for 3 days, they trust the result more than if a human blurts out an answer in 3 seconds. AI operates in a black box; the user cannot "feel" the effort. Consequently, they assume the AI just did a shallow autocomplete guess. Furthermore, beginners will *never* read a 1,000-line JSON trace log to verify the work.

CLOSEST_EXISTING_SOLUTION=ChatGPT's brief "Searched 3 sites" UI, or LangSmith/AgentOps developer tracing dashboards.

NEW_TO_OUR_PROJECT=The "Cryptographic Work Receipt" (A Nutrition Label for AI work). Every final deliverable generated by Courier is mandatorily prefixed with a 5-line standardized markdown table summarizing the ledger:
- Execution Time: 14m 22s
- Source Files Digested: 24 (1.2MB)
- External Systems Queried: 3 (Stripe, GitHub, Jira)
- Dead-ends Explored & Rejected: 2
- Cryptographic Signature: [SHA-256]
This gives the user an instant, 10-second heuristic of "Deep Work" without requiring them to read a single line of code or logs.

POSSIBLE_EXTERNAL_GAP=The industry provides either zero visibility (just the final text) or total visibility (complex developer trace graphs). There is no standard "Executive Summary of Effort" designed specifically to trigger human psychological trust.

CUSTOMER_VALUE=Instant psychological safety. The user immediately knows the AI didn't just guess; they can see the exact scope of the physical computational work that was expended to arrive at the answer.

MONEY_CONNECTION=Agencies delivering AI work to end-clients can use the "Work Receipt" to justify their billing. "Look at how much computational research went into this 2-page report." It shifts the perceived value from the length of the output to the depth of the process.

NOOB_CONNECTION=A beginner doesn't know what an API is, but they understand "I read 24 files and rejected 2 bad ideas in 14 minutes." It speaks their language.

RELIABILITY_CONNECTION=It forces the swarm to be honest. If the receipt says "Files read: 0", the human instantly knows the swarm hallucinated the answer from its weights and should be distrusted.

ROBOTICS_CONNECTION=A robot vacuum creates a final map showing exactly the square footage it covered. It doesn't just dock and say "I'm done, trust me."

WHY_THIS_COULD_BE_WRONG=Users might suffer from "banner blindness" and simply scroll past the receipt, ignoring it completely in favor of the actual content.

ONE_DAY_TEST=Write a script that parses `central_state.json` and automatically prepends a "Work Receipt" table to the top of an agent's final markdown output.

KILL_CONDITION=If A/B testing shows that non-technical users find the receipt confusing, annoying, or "clutter" rather than a trust-building signal, the idea should be dropped.

DO_NOT_REPEAT_FINGERPRINT=M07_WORK_RECEIPT_OBSERVABLE_EFFORT
