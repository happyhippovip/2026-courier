QUESTION=Who already owns the customer relationship and could profit by selling/using Courier?

WHAT_WE_ASSUMED=We assumed Courier should be a B2B SaaS sold directly to the end-users (e.g., selling directly to a startup CTO to help their engineering team).

WHAT_EVIDENCE_SAYS=Direct-to-SMB Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) is notoriously high. SMBs do not want to buy "AI Orchestration Software"; they want to buy finished services. Meanwhile, boutique agencies (Development shops, MSPs, SEO agencies, Compliance consultants) already own the trusted relationships with these end-clients, but struggle with low profit margins because human fulfillment labor is expensive.

CLOSEST_EXISTING_SOLUTION=White-label SaaS tools (like white-label SEO dashboards or PDF report generators). However, these tools only *identify* problems; they do not *fix* them. The agency still has to hire humans to do the actual remediation work.

NEW_TO_OUR_PROJECT="The Agency Execution Engine (White-Label Swarm)." Courier does not sell to the end-client. Instead, Courier sells to boutique agencies as a fulfillment engine. The agency sells a fixed-price $5,000 "AWS Cost Optimization" or "Codebase Modernization" project to their client. The agency then uses Courier to execute 80% of the manual labor for $50 in compute costs, pocketing the massive margin. Courier provides a white-labeled "Work Receipt" (M07) that the agency can hand to the client to prove the rigorous scope of work completed.

POSSIBLE_EXTERNAL_GAP=No major AI framework is explicitly positioning itself as a "White-Label Agency Fulfillment Backend." The market is focused on replacing agencies, but agencies have the one thing AI lacks: pre-existing trust and Master Service Agreements (MSAs) with traditional businesses. 

CUSTOMER_VALUE=For the Agency: Massive profit margin expansion and the ability to take on 10x more clients without hiring. For the End-Client: Faster turnaround times on fixed-price projects.

MONEY_CONNECTION=Courier bypasses the brutal direct-to-SMB sales cycle. By acquiring one successful agency partner, Courier indirectly acquires all 50 of that agency's clients. Courier takes a compute markup or a revenue-share on the fulfillment.

NOOB_CONNECTION=The end-client never even knows Courier exists. They just know their trusted local agency delivered a massive project flawlessly in 48 hours instead of 4 weeks.

RELIABILITY_CONNECTION=Agencies act as the perfect "Human in the Loop." Because it is their reputation on the line, the agency will rigorously review Courier's outputs before delivering them to the end-client, acting as a free QA layer that prevents the AI from directly damaging the end-client's systems.

ROBOTICS_CONNECTION=Auto manufacturers (like Magna Steyr) build cars for Mercedes and BMW. The consumer buys a BMW and trusts the BMW brand, even though a third-party factory robotics system actually built the chassis. 

WHY_THIS_COULD_BE_WRONG=Agencies might be terrified of liability. If Courier hallucinates and deletes an end-client's production database during a migration, the agency gets sued, not Courier. This fear might prevent agencies from adopting autonomous tools for write-heavy operations.

If they say yes, I will use my AI engine to do 90% of the work for you for $200. You keep the $1,800 margin." See if the financial incentive overcomes their skepticism.

KILL_CONDITION=If agencies refuse to use the tool because they cannot legally or ethically pass off AI-generated code under their existing client MSAs, this channel fails.
