FACTS=A normalization mismatch occurs when the statistical distribution of input data (observations like camera images) or output targets (actions) during deployment does not perfectly match the statistics used during training (e.g. mean/std normalization or min/max scaling). This is a well-known cause of deployment failure, causing erratic behavior (jitter) or complete task failure, because the latent representations are distorted or action denormalization executes incorrect physical magnitudes.

There is no widespread automated, cryptographically bound engine that mechanically guarantees the deployment normalization pipeline perfectly matches the evaluated pipeline.
