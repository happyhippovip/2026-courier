FACTS=A VLA is not just network weights; it includes an "executable policy" (metadata, decoding logic, gripper state conventions). A mismatch in action decoding (e.g. 1=open vs 1=closed, or global vs end-effector relative coordinates) causes silent failures where the neural network outputs correct *normalized* vectors, but the physical robot executes unintended, often dangerous, actions.
NEW_FINDING=Current research explicitly identifies the lack of "executable specifications" mapping model outputs to physical controllers as a pre-deployment safety hazard. Mismatches cause zero-shot success rates to plummet to 0% even if the model itself is highly capable. The industry recognizes this problem but currently relies on manual metadata configuration rather than an automated cryptographically enforced lock.
