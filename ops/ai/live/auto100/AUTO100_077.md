SOURCES_READ=Web search for "action chunking", "control frequency", and "timing mismatch" impact on VLA models.
FACTS=Action chunking is standard to prevent compounding errors, but introduces latency. "Timing mismatch" occurs when the physical robot's state drifts from the state observed when the chunk was generated. This leads to inter-chunk discontinuity (jerky motion) or intra-chunk inconsistency (silent failures, erratic movement). Real-time inference latency often forces a trade-off between chunk size and reactivity.
NEW_FINDING=Current state of the art addresses this via algorithmic fixes (adaptive chunking, asynchronous execution, recovery actions). No major framework guarantees at a systems level that the timing constraints and control loop frequency evaluated during training/simulation are cryptographically enforced on the deployment hardware. If edge compute degrades (e.g. thermal throttling), the timing mismatch silently invalidates the evaluation.
