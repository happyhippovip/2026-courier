FACTS=Calibration mismatch (discrepancies in camera extrinsics/intrinsics, actuator models, sensor fusion inputs) between training (evaluation) and deployment is a primary driver of sim-to-real transfer failure. It leads to "causally inconsistent" data where the VLA model hallucinates incorrect correlations because the real-world sensor data geometrically or radiometrically violates the evaluation envelope. 
NEW_FINDING=Current mitigation strategies involve either "calibration-free" architectures (forcing the model to infer its own camera geometry) or post-hoc uncertainty-aware control (triggering human intervention). There is a gap: rather than trying to make models robust to *any* calibration drift, there is high value in cryptographically verifying that the exact sensor extrinsics and hardware configuration evaluated in the pass-gate is identically bound to the deployment runtime.
