# Family 20: Pilot Candidate Profile & Promise Validation Interview Protocol

Status: READY FOR OPERATOR USE
Principle: Non-marketing qualitative validation of the core Courier promise.

---

## 1. Ideal Pilot Candidate Persona

An ideal first-cohort pilot user matches at least 4 of these 5 characteristics:
1. **High Context Loss**: Switches tasks or shuts down laptop often, losing in-flight state in existing CLI/agent workflows.
2. **Multi-Tool Fragmentation**: Juggles multiple windows/terminals/chats and manually relays outputs between them.
3. **Manual Human Relay Pain**: Frustrated by having to babysit agents and type "continue" or paste outputs from step A into step B.
4. **Restart & Resume Anxiety**: Anxious about long-running overnight jobs failing silently or re-running expensive steps from scratch upon reboot.
5. **Real Multi-Step Workloads**: Has a concrete, repetitive multi-step engineering goal (e.g. data sync -> transform -> test -> report) rather than casual toy chats.

---

## 2. Promise Validation Interview Questions

*These questions strictly evaluate whether Courier fulfilled its promise: "Brings you back exactly where you left off" — they deliberately avoid speculative feature requests.*

### Baseline (Before Using Courier):
1. *"When an AI tool works on a multi-step task and your computer restarts or disconnects, how much time do you spend figuring out what actually finished?"*
2. *"How often do you find yourself acting as the manual relay between two tools or steps?"*

### Day-1 Experience:
3. *"Did the Goal Contract make it clear what Courier was authorized to do before execution began?"*
4. *"Were you able to tell at a glance whether Courier was working (`ARBEITET`), needed your input (`BRAUCHT DICH`), or finished (`FERTIG`)?"*

### Next-Day Return (The Core Test):
5. *"When you opened your computer this morning, did Courier bring you back exactly where you left off?"*
6. *"Did you have to re-explain context or re-run completed steps?"*
7. *"Did you trust the verified results without needing to manually inspect every line of code?"*

### Economic Willingness:
8. *"If a tool completely eliminated your manual relay and preserved your context across every laptop sleep/restart, would you pay €20/month for that peace of mind?"*
