Price anchor:
EUR 390 one-time for the first three paid pilots.

Duration:
7 days.

Target:
one real AI-heavy workflow per customer.

What is sold:
- onboarding and workflow mapping;
- continuity/handoff setup around Courier;
- one defined real workflow;
- evidence-based pilot run;
- operator support during the pilot;
- end-of-pilot report with what worked, failed, and next recommendation.

What is NOT claimed:
- production-ready SaaS;
- zero bugs;
- fully autonomous operation before the technical gates prove it;
- guaranteed savings or revenue.

Customer-facing truth:
Courier is in a founding-pilot phase. The customer pays for a bounded setup + design-partner pilot, not for a falsely finished product.

Pilot success measures:
- time to resume work;
- number of manual interventions;
- handoff/state losses;
- successful continuation rate;
- setup/support time;
- next-day return;
- customer says whether the workflow is worth keeping;
- customer would pay again.

Commercial target:

This is a target, not a guarantee.
