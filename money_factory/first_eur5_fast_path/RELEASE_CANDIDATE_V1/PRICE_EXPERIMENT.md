---

## 2. Why €5? (The Rational Hypothesis)
1. **Low Friction Barrier**: €5 is within the standard "impulse buy" threshold for professional software engineers—comparable to the price of a coffee or a sub-single-hour utility tool.
2. **First Economic Validation**: The primary objective of this mission is NOT revenue maximization; it is establishing **the first nonzero legitimate commercial settlement (€5.00)** through an authoritative, verifiable loop.
3. **No Subscription Fatigue**: Developers consistently express resistance to recurring $2–$5/month micro-SaaS subscriptions for static CLI utilities. A one-time purchase eliminates ongoing commitment fear.

---

## 3. Explicit Unknowns (What We Do NOT Know)
- **True Willingness to Pay**: We do not have empirical historical conversion data for this specific product.
- **Price Elasticity**: Whether developers would pay €9, €15, or only accept free open-source software is an unverified market question.
- **Paywall Conversion Rate**: The percentage of landing page visitors who will complete checkout is an unvalidated hypothesis (hypothesized at 1.5%–3.0%).

---

## 4. Empirical Evaluation Criteria

### Definitive Success Signal:
- **One or more independently verified commercial purchases** of >= €5.00 settled in the Merchant of Record account without chargeback or dispute.
- This proves: Product exists + positioning communicates value + a stranger was willing to exchange real money for the utility.

### Failure Signal & Diagnostic Protocol:
- **Zero purchases after distribution test**:
  * **Crucial Rule**: Zero purchases does **NOT** immediately prove the product is useless or poorly built.
  * It indicates a breakdown in one of five distinct commercial funnel variables:
    1. **Distribution**: Not enough relevant developers saw the tool.
    2. **Trust**: Buyers hesitated to download a zip from an unfamiliar creator.
    3. **Positioning**: The headline did not clearly communicate the problem.
    4. **Audience**: The viewers were not active users of Cursor, Windsurf, or Cline.
    5. **Price Resistance**: The audience expects all developer CLI tools to be open-source.
- **Adjustment Rule**: If zero sales occur within 14 days of distribution launch, Chief evaluates the failure diagnostic before changing code or abandoning the hypothesis.
