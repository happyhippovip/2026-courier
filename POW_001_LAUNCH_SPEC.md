## 1. Overview

The **POW-001 Genesis Sponsorship** is a Bitcoin-native, fixed-supply sponsorship concept designed to bridge digital identity and physical operational hardware. 

The concept provides exactly **21 equal company blocks** on the next work machine deployed in the fleet. Each sponsor secures a permanent physical block on the hardware chassis coupled with an immutable digital genesis record in the project archive.

---

## 2. Rules of Engagement

The Genesis Sponsorship operates under strict, immutable parameters:

1. **Fixed Supply:** Exactly 21 blocks total. No additional blocks will ever be minted or added.
2. **Fair Allocation Limit:** Maximum of one (1) block per company/entity.
3. **Fixed Pricing:** Flat rate of 499 EUR equivalent, settled exclusively in Bitcoin (on-chain or Lightning).
4. **Order Policy:** First-come, first-served (FCFS). No auction dynamics or bidding wars.
5. **Egalitarian Structure:** No VIP tiers, preferred placements, or special privileges; all 21 blocks share equal dimensions and archival priority.
6. **Finality:** Once sold, blocks are permanent. No replacement blocks or secondary re-allocations by the issuing team.
7. **Physical-to-Digital Mapping:** Physical placement of company branding on the work machine directly corresponds to its digital Genesis position (Block #1 through Block #21).
8. **Permanent Genesis Archive:** A permanent digital Genesis archive records all 21 sponsor positions, verification hashes, and timestamps.
9. **Work Log Sequencing:** The public Work Log begins operational recording strictly after the Genesis cohort is completely filled and closed.
10. **Chain Distinction & Integrity:** The internal Genesis Chain/Ledger is an application-specific provenance record and is **NOT** the Bitcoin blockchain. It must never be misrepresented as a Bitcoin protocol fork or layer-1 consensus modification.

## 4. Physical Placement & Digital Genesis Archive

### Physical Machine Placement
- The work machine chassis features a dedicated 21-grid layout.
- Each sponsor block occupies identical surface area and branding dimensions.
- Position assignments (Blocks 1–21) follow sequential confirmation in the reservation registry.

### Permanent Digital Genesis Archive
- The Digital Genesis Archive serves as the persistent historical record.
- Every position records:
  - Genesis Position ID (`#01` to `#21`)
  - Company / Sponsor Name & Canonical URL
  - Vector logo / brand asset reference
  - Bitcoin Settlement TXID / Payment Proof
  - Cryptographic timestamp and proof hash
- The archive is hosted publicly and replicated across static distributed storage.

---

## 5. Work Log

The **Work Log** is the operational telemetry and audit ledger for the machine's ongoing work cycle.

- **Trigger:** The Work Log is activated immediately following the complete sale and confirmation of all 21 Genesis blocks.
- **Scope:** Tracks daily/weekly operational output, uptime, compute jobs, field maintenance, and performance milestones delivered by the machine.
- **Sponsor Visibility:** Genesis sponsors remain permanently credited across all published Work Log reports and milestones.

## 6. Human Gates & Operational Controls

To ensure strict compliance, security, and governance, the following actions are governed by mandatory **Human Approval Gates**:

- **Financial Actions & Treasury:** Wallet generation, address rotation, and payment validation require human verification.
- **Wallet Signing & Spending:** Any multisig transaction, fund transfer, or spending of collected BTC requires explicit human authorization.
- **Machine Purchase & Hardware Procurement:** Ordering the physical work machine chassis and fabrication materials requires explicit approval.
- **Contracts & Agreements:** Execution of sponsor agreements, terms of service, and corporate sponsor onboarding requires direct human sign-off.
- **Paid Advertising & Marketing:** Any capital allocation toward paid media, promotions, or public campaigns requires prior human review.
- **External Publication:** Final release of the Genesis Archive, marketing collateral, and public announcements to external channels must be cleared through human gates.
