## 2. Standardized Rapid-Response Playbooks (By Response Class)

### Scenario A: `POSITIVE_INTEREST` ("Sounds good, how do we start?")
- **Standard Action:** Provide direct 1-step intake link / payment checkout instructions within 15 minutes.
- **Template:**
  > "Great to connect. Here is how we get started with zero friction:
  > 1. Secure checkout link: [Stripe / Invoice Link] (€[Price])
  > 2. Intake: Simply share [sanitized file / sample log / repo link].
  > We deliver the complete verified package within [24-48h]. Looking forward to working together."

---

### Scenario B: `SCOPE_REQUEST` / `CUSTOM_REQUIREMENT` ("Can this cover X?")
- **Standard Action:** Define precise scope inclusion without expanding into unpaid custom consulting.
- **Template:**
  > "Yes — the standard €[Price] package specifically covers [exact scope]. For custom environments (e.g. multi-node clusters or custom DB adapters), we can include that within the €[Higher Tier] team tier. Happy to take a look at your specific setup."

---

### Scenario C: `PRICE_OBJECTION` ("Is there a free trial / discount?")
- **Standard Action:** Reiterate ROI and provide free open-source core / sample report while maintaining price integrity.
- **Template:**
  > "We keep the pricing fixed at €[Price] as a one-time founding tier (which pays for itself immediately by preventing 1 single unmonitored crash / token waste incident). You can inspect our public benchmark report first to evaluate the exact findings."

---

### Scenario D: `SECURITY_OR_DATA_PRIVACY_INQUIRY` ("Do we need to share API keys or production access?")
- **Standard Action:** Emphasize 100% local execution and zero-secret guarantee.
- **Template:**
  > "Zero production access or credentials required. The entire toolkit / audit operates strictly on local sanitized files and standard OS syscalls. No data leaves your local machine or CI/CD container."
