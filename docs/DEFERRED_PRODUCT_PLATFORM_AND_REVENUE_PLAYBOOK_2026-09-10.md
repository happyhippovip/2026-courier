Modernize it with:

- strong moderation, abuse prevention and user controls

## Preferred lawful revenue ladders

When the revenue phase becomes active, Courier should test the cheapest/fastest evidence first rather than betting on one giant product.

1. **Services / productized services** — fastest path to proof of willingness to pay, but human-gated for outbound contact until explicitly authorized.
2. **Digital products** — templates, tools, downloads, guides, small utilities, assets; low fulfillment cost.
3. **Micro-SaaS / software utility** — recurring revenue when a repeated painful workflow is proven.
4. **Paid community / memberships** — recurring value around belonging, expertise, coordination, events or exclusive utility.
5. **Marketplace / transaction fee** — only after supply/demand exists; avoid building empty marketplaces.
6. **Courses/events/coaching enablement** — platform may let creators monetize their own expertise.
7. **Affiliate/partnership revenue** — only transparent, policy-compliant offers that genuinely fit user intent; never spam or fake engagement.
8. **Advertising** — defer until meaningful scale; do not make surveillance/attention extraction the default business model.

For a future community/social product, prefer multiple complementary but non-exploitative revenue streams rather than dependence on one ad feed.

`RESEARCH -> CUSTOMER PAIN -> WTP EVIDENCE -> SMALLEST OFFER -> HUMAN GATE IF EXTERNAL -> SELL/TEST -> VERIFIED REVENUE -> UNIT ECONOMICS -> ITERATE OR KILL -> SCALE`

Before spending substantial build time, require at least one of:
- direct user/customer demand evidence
- credible search/market demand evidence

Never treat likes, model enthusiasm or generated market reports as revenue proof.

`GOAL: build a lawful repeatable path to >= EUR 50/day average verified revenue`

Success is NOT:
- 100 tasks completed
- website built
- app built
- content generated
- follower count

Success requires verified economic evidence.

Courier should prefer:
- low fixed cost
- low irreversible spend
- fast customer feedback

- high gross margin
- low founder relay time

- fake engagement/account farming
- spam
- deceptive marketing
- quota/limit evasion
- unauthorized outreach
- hidden affiliate incentives
- dark patterns

## Product-selection scorecard

When several future products compete for attention, score them before dispatch:

- real pain / urgency
- willingness to pay evidence
- time to first EUR
- path to >EUR 50/day average
- recurring potential
- distribution access
- gross margin
- legal/compliance complexity
- moderation/trust burden
- autonomous operability by Courier
- human-gate frequency
- model/tool cost
- defensibility / learning advantage
- founder interest and strategic fit

Do not automatically prefer the most ambitious idea. Prefer the idea with the best expected verified outcome per time/cost/risk.
