## Founder operating objective

The founder should not have to repeatedly ask:
- "what next?"
- "continue?"
- "which worker?"
- "which prompt?"
- "did we already do this?"

Human time should be reserved for:
- strategy
- genuine money decisions
- safety/permission decisions
- real customer/pilot decisions
- final convergence/release gates

Normal worker routing, result collection and continuation should not depend on the founder acting as a message bus.

## Wall/company capacity philosophy

Logical wall size is not active heavy-process count.

The user-facing product may support selectable wall sizes, but actual admission must be resource-, provider-, dependency- and budget-aware.

Preferred behavior:
- smooth verified throughput
- many logical tasks
- few heavy jobs
- no idle AI polling
- no duplicate source writers
- result reuse
- truthful WAITING/GUARDED/IDLE states

A large wall is useful only when it produces more distinct verified work with less human relay.

## Cost philosophy

Treat provider/API spend as an operating resource.

Spend when it:
- closes a real blocker
- produces required evidence
- reduces real risk
- shortens human coordination
- advances a real pilot/release gate

Stop spending when work is:
- duplicate
- unchanged
- speculative
- idle
- outside the authorized critical path

Never keep paid workers alive simply because capacity or budget remains.

## Sleep / unattended work

Before sleep:
- durable queue exists
- claims/results survive session loss
- writer ownership is singular
- resource guard is active
- heavy concurrency is bounded
- no payment/permission/destructive task is waiting for silent approval
- permanent worker prompt is available
- TRUE_IDLE workers can stop rather than burn tokens

Long unattended rounds may run up to the bounded policy limit when safe.
