# Category Creation Lab

## CURRENT_LANGUAGE_PROBLEM
"Multi-agent ledger orchestration" and "stateful execution" are engineering terms. Business buyers do not buy architecture; they buy insurance, productivity, and control. If we explain *how* it works (Ledger, JSONL transcripts), we lose the customer. We need language that focuses on the *absence of failure*.

## EVALUATION OF TOP 5

1. **AI Continuity**
   * Searches? Moderate (disaster recovery adjacency).
   * Comprehension? High. Everyone understands "business continuity".
   * Budget? Yes (IT/Risk).
   * Crowded? Low.
   * Courier match? Perfect (crash recovery, ledger).
   * 30s Demo? Kill a process, watch it resume exactly where it left off.
   * Repeatable? "It makes sure the AI doesn't lose its work."
   * **Headline:** "When your AI drops the ball, Courier picks it up. Zero lost work."

2. **Autonomous Workforce Management**
   * Searches? Rising rapidly.
   * Comprehension? High (HR/Ops analogy).
   * Budget? Yes (Operations/Software).
   * Crowded? Medium.
   * Courier match? High (managing different providers like Gemini, Claude).
   * 30s Demo? Show the dashboard of 3 different AIs assigned distinct tasks without duplicating work.
   * Repeatable? "It's the HR software for our AI bots."
   * **Headline:** "The management platform for your AI employees."

3. **AI Task Governance**
   * Searches? High (Enterprise compliance).
   * Comprehension? High.
   * Budget? High (Security/Compliance).
   * Crowded? High (but mostly focused on data privacy, not execution).
   * Courier match? High (sandbox, approvals, physical owner).
   * 30s Demo? Show an AI attempting a heavy destructive action and being blocked by the governor.
   * Repeatable? "It stops the AI from doing things it shouldn't."
   * **Headline:** "Control exactly what your AI does, across every device and model."

4. **AI Reliability**
   * Searches? High.
   * Comprehension? Very High.
   * Budget? Yes (Engineering/IT).
   * Crowded? High (mostly observability tools like LangSmith).
   * Courier match? Good.
   * 30s Demo? Show a complex 20-step process succeeding despite 3 network drops.
   * Repeatable? "It makes the AI stop failing randomly."
   * **Headline:** "Stop babysitting your AI. Get guaranteed execution for complex workflows."

5. **Outcome Orchestration**
   * Searches? Low.
   * Comprehension? Medium.
   * Budget? Medium (Project management).
   * Crowded? Low.
   * Courier match? High (Ledger goals vs tasks).
   * 30s Demo? Enter a high-level goal, watch multiple AIs break it down and deliver the outcome.
   * Repeatable? "You tell it what you want, it manages the AIs to get it done."
   * **Headline:** "Don't manage AI models. Manage the outcomes they deliver."
