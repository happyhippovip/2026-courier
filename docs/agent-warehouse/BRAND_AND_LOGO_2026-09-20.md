# Brand & Logo Handoff

Date: 2026-09-20  
Status: design concept / naming decision still open

## Current logo concept

A green premium technology/eco-style emblem built from **four infinity symbols** arranged around a central point.

Visual intent:
- four infinity loops;
- green / forest-green palette;
- clean light background;
- modern, premium, trustworthy;
- recognizable as app icon and company mark;
- suitable for website, dashboard, social profile and future product packaging.

A generated concept image exists in the working conversation. The binary image itself has **not** been committed here yet; this document preserves the design brief so it can be reproduced or converted into proper SVG/vector artwork.

## Rights note

The generated concept can be used as a design direction, but final commercial branding should be checked for:
- conflicting trademarks;
- confusing similarity with existing logos;
- domain availability;
- final rights/ownership in commissioned design assets.

Do not claim exclusive ownership of the generic infinity symbol itself. Protect the **specific brand expression, wordmark, files, code, texts and registered marks** where applicable.
