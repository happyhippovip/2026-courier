# Brand & Logo Handoff

Date: 2026-09-20  
Status: design concept / naming decision still open

## Current logo concept

A green premium technology/eco-style identity for **Courier Symphony**, with infinity as the recurring symbol.

Core logo direction:
- green / emerald / teal;
- infinity motif;
- premium, modern, trustworthy;
- clear Courier Symphony wordmark.

Campaign/social variants may additionally use a Maldives/tropical direction with palm, island, lagoon or wave elements while keeping the brand recognizable.

Visual intent:
- four infinity loops;
- green / forest-green palette;
- clean light background;
- modern, premium, trustworthy;
- recognizable as app icon and company mark;
- suitable for website, dashboard, social profile and future product packaging.

A generated concept image exists in the working conversation. The binary image itself has **not** been committed here yet; this document preserves the design brief so it can be reproduced or converted into proper SVG/vector artwork.

## Naming decision

**Confirmed product/project name: Courier Symphony.**

"Corineria Symphonie" was an earlier/incorrect logo-generation wording and should not be used as the product name.

Do not silently rename code, domains, package names or legal documents away from Courier Symphony.

Recommended decision record before launch:
- legal company name;
- product name;
- trademark candidate;
- domain;
- app/store display name;
- GitHub repository name;
- logo wordmark spelling;
- German vs English spelling.

## Rights note

The generated concept can be used as a design direction, but final commercial branding should be checked for:
- conflicting trademarks;
- confusing similarity with existing logos;
- domain availability;
- final rights/ownership in commissioned design assets.

Do not claim exclusive ownership of the generic infinity symbol itself. Protect the **specific brand expression, wordmark, files, code, texts and registered marks** where applicable.
