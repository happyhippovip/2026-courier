## 1. Current Platform Economics & Rules Verification

Every metric and platform rule has been audited against current official documentation and verified public market data:

### 1.1 YouTube Partner Program (YPP)
- **Eligibility Thresholds**:
  - *Tier 1 (Fan Funding only)*: 500/1,000 subscribers + 3,000 long-form watch hours (365d) OR 3 million valid Shorts views (90d).
  - *Tier 2 (Full Ad Revenue Sharing)*: 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 long-form watch hours (365d) OR 10 million valid Shorts views (90d).
- **Shorts Monetization Economics**:
  - *Verified RPM*: $0.08 to $0.20 per 1,000 views in Tier-1 countries (US, UK, Germany, Canada); $0.01 to $0.05 in Tier-3 countries.
  - *Strategic Impact*: 1,000,000 Shorts views generates only ~$80 to ~$200 in ad pool revenue. Relying solely on Shorts ad revenue for initial cash flow is economically unviable.
- **Reused Content Policy**:
  - YouTube explicitly denies YPP entry to channels that compile third-party clips, memes, or re-upload content with minimal edits.
  - Content must possess substantial original commentary, transformative educational value, or unique synthesis.
- **AI / Synthetic Media Disclosure**:
  - YouTube Studio provides a mandatory toggle for "meaningfully altered or synthetically generated" realistic content (e.g. realistic synthetic voice cloning, altered real-world events).
  - Disclosure is **NOT** required for obvious animations, graphic cards, or AI used for brainstorming, drafting scripts, and generating captions.

### 1.2 TikTok Creator Rewards Program
- **Eligibility Thresholds**:
  - Minimum 18 years old, 10,000 followers, and at least 100,000 views in the last 30 days.
  - Available only in supported regions (US, UK, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Brazil).
- **The Critical 1-Minute Rule**:
  - **Videos under 60 seconds earn exactly $0.00 from Creator Rewards.**
  - Eligible videos must be $\ge 60$ seconds in length, with RPMs typically ranging from $0.40 to $1.00 for qualified Tier-1 views.
- **Originality & AI Rules**:
  - Stitches, Duets, and re-uploaded videos are disqualified from rewards.
  - Purely automated push-button AI content without original creator input is prohibited. AI-assisted workflows (with human editorial direction and original scripts) remain eligible, provided mandatory AI-content disclosure toggles are set when synthetic media/voice is utilized.

### 1.3 Day-1 Monetization Reality: Direct Affiliate vs. Platform Ad Checks
- **Verified Insight**: Because YPP requires 1,000 subs / 4,000 hrs / 10M Shorts views and TikTok requires 10,000 followers, **Day-1 cash flow must originate from direct monetization**:
  - SaaS & Software Affiliate Programs (e.g. Make, Zapier, n8n hosting, AI tools): $15–$50+ per referral or 20–30% recurring commissions.
  - A single qualified software signup from 2,000 targeted viewers yields more immediate revenue than 200,000 unmonetized Shorts views.
